Subscription & billing

Common questions about your account, plans, payments and cancellations.

What subscription plans do you offer?

We offer three tiers: Starter, Professional and Enterprise. Each plan includes increasing storage limits, API access, priority support and team collaboration features.

How does billing work?

Billing occurs automatically at the start of each cycle. You receive an invoice by email after each payment.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancel any time from your account settings. Access continues until the end of the current billing period.
